Web Site Critiques

Trader Joe's - https://www.traderjoes.com/

My first impression of the Trader Joe's website was that I really liked the colors and pictures. The website had a vibe that matched the fall vibe that matched the current season. The colors and images made the website feel warm and intriguing.

The Trader Joe's website organizes itself by having a navigation menu on the left-hand side of page. The pictures are placed throughout the site by the text they are showing to make it more interactive. The products have their own tab on the navigation menu which makes them easy to find. Overall I would say the website is very organized.

The structure of the website makes sense to me because they have the information organized. The navigation menu makes it seem pretty straightforward to me. I did not find it difficult to use.

I think that the pictures and the layout of the website make it more user-friendly. Sometimes the pictures will be a certain food that they are advertising, which is nice to see what the product looks like. The pictures also catch my attention while I am looking through the website.

The Trader Joe's website loaded almost instantly for me.

I thought that the text color and size were very good on their website. Everything was easy to read and I didn't have any trouble reading anything. The background and text colors worked well together.

The website was fairly easy to navigate. The menu on the left-hand side made it easy to find things. I didn't have any problems finding what I was looking for.

The Trader Joe's website has a consistent color scheme and look throughout its pages. I think they do a good job of making everything feel like it belongs to the same place.

When the browser gets smaller the navigation menu disappears and has to be opened manually. This makes the website work well on a smaller screen and still keeps things organized. I think this is a good feature for people using the site on smaller devices.

One thing that I thought was exceptional was the seasonal design of the website. The way they had the fall colors, and different fall products as the focus felt really cool to me.
